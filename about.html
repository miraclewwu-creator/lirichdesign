About Us

LIRICH STUDIO is a China-based multidisciplinary design practice working across architecture, interior design, commercial strategy and hospitality. We create hotels, resorts, restaurants, private residences and mixed-use destinations in which business ambition, cultural identity and everyday experience become one coherent idea.

Our work begins before form. We study a project's market, audience, operations and long-term value, then translate that intelligence into planning, architecture, interiors, materiality and atmosphere. This continuity helps clients make clearer decisions and gives every detail a shared purpose.

Rooted in China and open to the world, we bring an Eastern sensibility to contemporary design: balance over display, depth over novelty, and close attention to light, proportion, nature and craft.

From Strategy to Experience

We believe hospitality is not a look, but a way of caring for people. A successful hotel must work as rigorously as it feels effortless, from arrival and circulation to guestrooms, dining, service and back-of-house. We focus on purpose, empathy and lived experience rather than surface alone, designing for guests, operators, owners and the place itself. The result should feel intuitive and memorable while remaining operationally clear and capable of maturing over time.

Our multidisciplinary model allows us to treat every project as a complete ecosystem. Commercial positioning informs the spatial concept; architecture establishes order and context; interiors define rhythm, comfort and identity; and furniture, art and styling bring the story to a human scale. Working across these layers does not mean imposing one signature. It means protecting one clear vision through a complex process while respecting the culture, climate and character of each place.

In private residences, luxury is not measured by excess. It is the quiet precision with which a home supports privacy, family life, entertaining, collecting and rest. We design spaces that can grow with their owners and hold both daily rituals and important occasions. For commercial and hospitality clients, the same thinking creates destinations with emotional distinction, operational clarity and lasting relevance.

At LIRICH STUDIO, trust is built through judgment, discipline and responsibility. We listen closely, test decisions against the brief and business case, and remain attentive from the first strategy conversation to the final detail. The result is design with a clear point of view and a practical foundation: distinctly Chinese in cultural confidence, international in standard, and made to create value for the people who live, work and gather there.
