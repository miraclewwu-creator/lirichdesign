Services

Architecture | Interior Design | FF&E & Procurement | Project Management | Antiques & Art | Furniture & Lighting

LIRICH STUDIO offers an integrated service spanning architecture, interiors, sourcing and delivery. Each commission is guided by context, craft and the way people will inhabit the space.

01

Architecture

We develop architecture from a close reading of site, climate and context. Proportion, light, material and movement are resolved as one coherent whole, creating places that are enduring, purposeful and unmistakably their own.

02

Interior Design

We shape interiors around the people who use them. Spatial planning, materiality, colour and bespoke detailing are carefully layered to create rooms that feel intuitive, personal and quietly distinctive.

03

FF&E & Procurement

From one-of-a-kind pieces to complete FF&E packages, we source furniture, lighting, art, textiles and finishes through our trusted international network. Specification, purchasing, logistics and installation are managed as one coordinated process.

04

Project Management

We provide clear oversight from early planning through final handover, coordinating budgets, programmes, consultants, contractors and site execution. Close communication and rigorous follow-through keep every decision aligned with the design intent.

05

Antiques & Art

We source antiques, artworks and collectible objects that bring depth, character and a sense of time to a space. Each piece is selected for its provenance, material presence and relationship to the wider interior.

06

Furniture & Lighting

We design bespoke furniture and lighting when a project calls for something singular. Each piece is developed in dialogue with the interior, balancing proportion, comfort, function and craft from first sketch to final installation.
